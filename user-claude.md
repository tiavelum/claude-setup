Decision issues: Before opening an issue labelled `tiavelum-decides`, have a `checker` that did not see the work read its draft without opening any link, and report what it had to guess and what could be removed without losing anything it needed; rewrite the draft once from that report.

Attribution in descriptions: In the description of an issue or a pull request that has collapsed sections, put the attribution lines at the end of its top part, before the first collapsed section, since nothing may follow those sections.
